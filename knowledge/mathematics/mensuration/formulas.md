# Mensuration Formulas

## Cuboid

Volume = length x width x height.

Total surface area = 2(lw + wh + lh).

Lateral surface area = 2h(l + w).

## Cube

Volume = side^3.

Total surface area = 6 side^2.

Lateral surface area = 4 side^2.

Space diagonal = side x sqrt(3).

## Cylinder

Volume = pi r^2 h.

Curved surface area = 2 pi r h.

Total surface area = 2 pi r(r + h).

## Cone

Volume = 1/3 pi r^2 h.

Curved surface area = pi r l.

Total surface area = pi r(l + r).

Slant height l = sqrt(r^2 + h^2).

## Sphere

Volume = 4/3 pi r^3.

Surface area = 4 pi r^2.

## Hemisphere

Volume = 2/3 pi r^3.

Curved surface area = 2 pi r^2.

Total surface area = 3 pi r^2.
