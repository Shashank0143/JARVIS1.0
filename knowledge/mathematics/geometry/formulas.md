# Geometry Formulas

## Lines and Angles

Sum of angles on a straight line = 180 degrees.

Sum of angles around a point = 360 degrees.

Sum of interior angles of a triangle = 180 degrees.

Sum of interior angles of an n-sided polygon = (n - 2) x 180 degrees.

Each interior angle of a regular n-sided polygon = ((n - 2) x 180) / n.

## Triangle

Area of triangle = 1/2 x base x height.

Heron's formula: area = sqrt(s(s-a)(s-b)(s-c)), where s = (a+b+c)/2.

Pythagorean theorem: a^2 + b^2 = c^2 for a right triangle.

## Rectangle and Square

Area of rectangle = length x width.

Perimeter of rectangle = 2(length + width).

Area of square = side^2.

Perimeter of square = 4 x side.

Diagonal of square = side x sqrt(2).

## Circle

Circumference = 2 pi r.

Area = pi r^2.

Diameter = 2r.

Arc length = theta / 360 x 2 pi r.

Sector area = theta / 360 x pi r^2.
